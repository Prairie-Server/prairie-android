## Prairie rebrand

This repository uses the Prairie identity for app names, package identifiers,
deep links, LAN service names, and brand assets. Distributed Prairie builds must
not present themselves as official Silo builds or use Silo brand assets as their
identity.

Factual references remain permitted when they are truthful and non-confusing,
for example: "Prairie is a fork of Silo" or "compatible with Silo servers" when
that compatibility exists. Such references must not imply endorsement by Silo
Media L.L.C. or the upstream Silo project.

in this repository replace the upstream Silo brand assets. They are not Silo
Media assets and should not be treated as permission to use any Silo marks.

See the `NOTICE` files under the Android resource directories for the local
brand-asset notice included with packaged resources.
